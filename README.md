# PaperFold Gallery

Landmark papers from eight fields, to fold and unfold: **https://chenxiachan.github.io/paperfold-gallery/**

Each page is the static PaperFold reader holding one paper at five levels, from the full text to a one-screen map, in English, Chinese and German. It opens at the Topic level; pinch, or press 1 to 5, to go down to every word.

Papers belong to their authors and keep their licenses (all Creative Commons), named under each title. PaperFold is not affiliated with the authors; the levels, links and translations were made by AI. An author who would like a paper taken down can [open an issue](https://github.com/chenxiachan/paperfold-gallery/issues).

`fixes/` holds the proofreading: corrections to the AI's levels and translations, checked against each paper, which the build puts over the model's text. It will not build a paper without them.
